Brush alphas

Put greyscale pictures (.png or .jpg) in this folder, or in folders inside it,
to print with them from the Standard, Clay, Inflate and Layer brushes. White
prints the most and black not at all. Square pictures work best; one that is
not square is centred, with black where it does not reach.

The app lists them under the Alpha heading of the brush settings, by file
name. Use Rescan Folder there after adding pictures while the app is open.
Import Alpha... copies a picture from anywhere into the Imported folder here.
